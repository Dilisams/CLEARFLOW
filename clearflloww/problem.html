The challenge

Water stress and sanitation risk are connected problems.

In Enugu communities, poor water access, borehole failure, leakages, contamination, blocked drainages, poor waste management, sewage overflow, flooding, and hygiene issues compound into public-health risk.

Water Problems

Poor water access

Many communities depend on irregular supply, distant water points, and manual reporting channels that delay repairs.

Leakages and borehole failure

Small failures become expensive because teams lack live visibility into status, pressure changes, and maintenance history.

Contamination

Dirty water reports and quality indicators need faster escalation before disease clusters emerge.

Sanitation Problems

Blocked drainages and flooding

Waste-filled drains intensify stormwater flooding and contaminate homes, markets, and public spaces.

Poor waste management

Illegal dumping and weak collection visibility create recurring hotspots around roads and drainage channels.

Hygiene and sewage issues

Overflowing sewage, limited toilet access, and poor hygiene data raise preventable disease risk.
