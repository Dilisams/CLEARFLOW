How ClearFlow works

From community signal to accountable response.

ClearFlow creates a shared operating picture for water and sanitation by blending smart monitoring, citizen reporting, AI prioritization, government workflow, and impact tracking.

Smart monitoring

Capture pH, turbidity, supply uptime, leakage, drainage, waste, sewage, and flood-risk signals.

Community reporting

Residents submit water and sanitation issues with location, severity, photo, and GPS context.

AI prioritization

Risk scoring escalates contamination, leak, flood, sewage, and hygiene threats based on combined evidence.

Government response

Admins assign status, track resolution, export reports, and coordinate WASH responders.

Impact tracking

Progress metrics show improved access, lower leakage, reduced flooding, hygiene gains, and SDG alignment.
